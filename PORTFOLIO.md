## LinkedIn draft
I built MindType Studio to explore typing behaviour as a data science research problem.

The prototype measures keyboard timing, pauses and corrections without additional sensors, compares two neutral typing exercises, and presents a minimal dashboard. Typed text stays in the browser.

The project includes opt-in dataset collection, exploratory analysis and participant-separated evaluation comparing Ridge regression, random forest and a mean baseline. The default demo reports actual typing measurements; real-world strain prediction requires real labelled data and evaluation.

My biggest learning: a working interface and high synthetic-data accuracy do not establish a validated mental-health model.

Stack: Python, JavaScript, HTML/CSS, pandas, NumPy and scikit-learn.
